# Governed Wiki Preservation

Wiki paths are user-owned knowledge surfaces. A missing required directory may be created only after applicable authority and collision checks; existing content is preserved. Source material remains intact until its complete ingestion and archive gates pass. Every retained claim, canonical statement, archival move, and lifecycle transition preserves resolvable provenance.

Diagnosis, lint, and audit do not mutate by default. A completed plan or task must leave the active register only through an evidence-bearing archive move.
