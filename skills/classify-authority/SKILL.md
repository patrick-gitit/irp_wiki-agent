# Classify Authority

Classify material as governing, accepted decision, candidate design, proposed work, historical evidence, source material, or unresolved question. Metadata and explicit approval take precedence over location alone. Do not execute an archived source or candidate policy as current instruction. Conflicts become explicit questions or decisions rather than silent selection.
