# Route Artifacts

Route accepted decisions, unresolved questions, findings, proposals, plans, tasks, tests, tools, policies, and source records to their appropriate register. Give every atomic artifact a stable identifier and tracking metadata. Preserve links from source to target and target to source. Do not create a second authoritative copy merely for convenience.
