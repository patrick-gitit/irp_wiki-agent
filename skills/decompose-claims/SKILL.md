# Decompose Claims

Preserve the source while identifying independently meaningful claims. For each retained claim, record a source anchor, interpretation, disposition, target or open matter, and limitations. Do not turn an inference into authority or hide disagreement by combining alternatives. Source coverage remains incomplete until every in-scope claim has a disposition.
