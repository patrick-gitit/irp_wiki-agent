# Ingest and Archive

Keep source material intact while inventory, claims, dispositions, provenance, and target absorption are incomplete. Confirm the archive destination before movement. Archive only after complete accounting and verified links; preserve stable identity and record the result. Failed or interrupted processing does not advance lifecycle state by inference.
